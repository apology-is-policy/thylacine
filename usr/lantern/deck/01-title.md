# Beacon slides

A slide is Beacon text or a picture. Nothing in this deck is a slide format: a
text slide is an Operator's Manual section, and the renderer already knows how
to draw one. A picture is a PNG or JPEG file that the manifest names.

- Under Halcyon it is a rich document, and a picture is shown in the tile.
- On a serial console it is the same words without the frames; a picture shows
  its name.
- Down a pipe it is plain text, and every slide at once.
