== why ramfs.cpio has a different sha256 in every leg (see <leg>-inputs.sha256)
    Each `tools/build.sh kernel` mints a fresh libsodium-randomized pool key
    (build_stratum_pool_fixture) and copies it into the ramfs at bin/system.key;
    mkcpio zeroes mtimes, so the key is the varying member. The probe itself is
    proven per leg by extraction: every non-probe leg's baked probe is
    byte-identical to the controls' (same_probe), and M13's differs.
