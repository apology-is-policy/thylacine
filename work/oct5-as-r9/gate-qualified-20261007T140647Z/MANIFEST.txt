WHY THIS DIRECTORY EXISTS, and why it is not boot-inputs/.
The gate's own build REPLACED three of the four inputs a boot reads: ramfs, the
pool snapshot and the key were rebaked at 14:11Z, after the post-build
preservation point at 14:08:52Z. So the morning's curated set
(private-owner-qualified-kernel/) is NOT this set, and its MANIFEST says as much:
its ramfs was already LOST when the gap was found, so it pins what the kernel and
pool were but cannot be booted as a set. astra named that explicitly on 0161 t49
-- "private-owner-qualified-kernel alone is not its ramfs/pool/key replacement" --
and asked that these bytes be excluded from rolling pruning until they sat in a
durable curated set. This is that set: promoted out of
boot-inputs/run-20261007T140647Z-postgate by `git mv`, every file re-verified
against the committed HASHES.txt AFTER the move, and marked PINNED so the
runbook's pruner would skip it even if it were still under boot-inputs/.
