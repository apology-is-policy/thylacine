Kernel integration refinement before implementation:
1. Embed16-byte close_work in Loom (still one buddy page), global close queue
   under existing retire lock. Start at actual handle-close with a caller ref,
   latch through existing leaf exec function, take queue root under Q and wake
   after unlock. Zero-ref enqueue calls a separate bare close notification.
2. Retirer processes one close quantum plus one final-destroy item per iteration.
   No Q lock across Ll, native progress, callbacks, free or wake. Finish DROP
   means one loom_unref outside Q. PARKED nodes retain root with no runnable link.
3. Distinguish quiet internal step/entry-put/collection from external callbacks.
   Public CONNECT driver/teardown exit, constructor rollback and raw-owner release
   notify; public indexed entry return/removal notify. Private retirement uses
   quiet variants; these still wake the ordinary ring rendez but do not kick
   their own close queue. No suppression of concurrent external RUNNING kicks.
4. A close quantum selects one LIVE nonretired nonbusy owner, retaining its entry,
   runs the actual CONNECT cancellation step, drops its internal entry pin, then
   attempts quiescent consumer detach/discard/collection. Scan eligible work to
   decide AGAIN; no eligible work ->WAIT on actual external prerequisite events.
   No transport readiness needed for abort. Busy users/drivers/installer/fixed
   and unindexed requests prevent complete; no duration or peer-based reclamation.
5. Do not reap scope metadata from the close worker while a provisional FID
   constructor may still hold it. Once protocol storage and requests are empty,
   release the queue root; caller/borrow refs still retain the ring. Final
   zero-ref cleanup removes unused FIDs first, then may reap genuinely RETIRED
   scopes after checking live phase, zero pins and zero parent/reserved fields.
   READY/ABORTING still fail closed. This preserves existing transaction lifetime
   rather than deleting a caller's provisional reservation during close.
6. fixed_release must keep fixed_count nonzero through physical buffer settlement.
   Unlink its exact reservation before unlocked settlement but decrement count
   only afterwards, then kick while its retained Loom ref remains. A new acquire
   is refused after close. Existing overlap exclusion before close needs the
   reservation left linked until settlement, or a separate finishing count;
   choose a separate service_fixed_settling counter if unlink stays early.
