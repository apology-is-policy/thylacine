Astra single-agent self-review: simulated local wait coordinator, October10.
Uninstalled Rust-only draft. No independent audit or native sleeping-thread proof.

This step composes the existing creator-local retirement pulse with a bounded
wait decision; it is not a complete task executor. turn performs at most one
bounded retirement sweep and one wait. It checks an absolute deadline before
work, respects remaining pulse work, arms interest, rechecks external readiness
and time, then checks local ownership pulses last. Nonblocking readiness/time
callbacks can drop local owners without losing that pulse. No queue RefCell borrow
survives into event-source methods. A guard disarms on all paths after a successful
arm, including early readiness/deadline, wait error and ordinary/spurious wake.
Failed arm has no subscription to disarm. Caller handles reported errors; no
retry loop or assumed success. Existing drivers and C code are unchanged.

WaitSource is an adapter contract, not an implemented native primitive. Its
subscription ticket must detect an event after the readiness sample; checking
that ticket and parking must be atomic at the actual event source. Events must
remain pending until serviced, including across disarm; readiness observation is
not service completion. wait may not run local owner-drop callbacks between the
final pulse sample and parking. Creator-thread ownership excludes local drops
while that creator is blocked. A real adapter must separately prove those facts,
registration cleanup and event persistence. The deterministic host source tests
the caller protocol but cannot prove an OS atomic wait implementation.

The deadline is an unchanged absolute monotonic value; None means no deadline.
Spurious wake, changed generation and ordinary wake notify bounded progress and
return control to the caller. Deadline outcome only reports scheduling expiry;
it never fabricates completion, cancels ownership or releases active storage.
Per-request deadline registration and scope-wide cancellation policy are next.
This also needs a runnable-task predicate: the eventual adapter must include
pending runnable/CQ/backend work, not just a transport-ready flag. No private
syscall integration, event source, timer heap or full executor is installed.
