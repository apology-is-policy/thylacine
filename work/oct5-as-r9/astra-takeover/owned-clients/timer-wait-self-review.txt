Astra single-agent self-review: Future/retirement timer composition, October10.
Uninstalled Rust draft, simulated sources only; not an independent audit.

Future Read/Write wrappers forward prepublication absolute-deadline configuration
and explicit scope-wide cancel to their owned typed request. Cancellation does
not fabricate readiness or invoke task Wakers. Futures still poll Pending until
an authenticated terminal result or actual driver failure. A successful WRITE
completion after cancellation remains successful, not rewritten as timeout.

The generic retirement queue forwards expiry/minimum for both polling and Future
drivers. Its independent rotating timer cursor includes attached AND retiring
drivers; retirement progress cannot repeatedly reset or bias timer visits. Bounds
are min(ring_budget,capacity)*min(fixed_budget,64) owner-cell visits per expiry
call. The exact deadline minimum scans at most64drivers*64cells under the current
two Retirable implementations. No growing timer queue, callbacks, allocations
or new native ownership capacity is introduced. Backend aborts retain their
nonblocking/nonreentrant contract; no user Waker is invoked beneath the manager.

The timed wait adapter first honors an expired outer caller deadline, then does
one bounded expiry scan. The wait deadline is min(outer,all remaining request
deadlines). Request expiry found after the scan or while arming produces Retry,
allowing the next bounded scan. Partial/zero budgets do not turn due requests
into an outer timeout or permission to sleep. Positive budgets are the caller's
fair-progress obligation. Equal request/outer deadline keeps outer timeout policy;
outer timeout does not imply scope abort, storage release or completion. A real
wait wake returns control first; a subsequent timed turn handles due requests.

Publication during arm signals the last local recheck, preventing a sleep with
an obsolete deadline. EventSource still owns the atomic check/park and persistent
readiness contract; this code does not supply a native wake primitive. Caller
runnable/CQ dispatch is not implemented by this wait coordinator. No kernel/SQ
changes. C remains unchanged; this is only Rust timer/retirement composition.
