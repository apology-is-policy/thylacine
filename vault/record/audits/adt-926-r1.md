## Scope

The at-exit close window, the `thread_count == 1` gate, and the userspace
command-substitution consumer that motivated it. One Opus prosecutor,
ground-truthed against a clean build + full suite + boot, plus a concurrent
self-audit that independently confirmed the gate.

## Convergence

CLEAN; NOT dirty (0 P0/P1, the fixes comment-only). The prosecutor traced
and then WITHDREW the headline risk — sleeping in the wrong state — after
following every interleaving: at the close the thread is still RUNNING, so a
sleeping 9P clunk is legal, and a concurrent group-terminate mid-clunk wakes
the stack rendez and returns `SLEEP_INTR` while RESTORING `THREAD_RUNNING`
(the "current is not RUNNING" assert guards loop ENTRY only).

The verified-sound set also covers the reaper race, the lockless
`thread_count` read, double-free, the I-7 ordering inversion, and the
userspace drain-before-reap.
