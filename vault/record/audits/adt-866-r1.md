`idle_in_wfi` accuracy, the steal/pick invariant asserts) plus the #864
HMP foundation (`select_target_cpu`, `ready_on`, DTB capacity, util,

## Verdict

**Clean: 0 P0 / 1 P1 / 0 P2 / 5 P3.** Not dirty — P1+P2 = 1, far under
the threshold, and the F1 fix is a one-flag set rather than a structural
restructure. No round 2.

## Verified sound — do not re-prosecute without a new vector

- **Behavior preservation is byte-identical.** The prosecutor diffed the
  pre-#864 `ready()` against
  `ready() -> select_target_cpu (returns self) -> ready_on(self)` step by
  step: identical validate, lock, in-tree extinction, insert, unlock,
  notify. The only deltas are two harmless field reads and a benign
  reordering of extinction-on-failure validations.
- **Cross-CPU `ready_on` is deadlock-free.** It holds exactly one
  `CpuSched` lock and never nests, so it cannot cycle with `try_steal`
  (trylocks peers), `sched()` (own lock only) or
  `sched_remove_if_runnable` (one at a time). `g_cpu_sched` and its lock
  are file-private, so no external caller arrives holding one.
- **`util` has no race**: single-writer by [[inv-i21]] — accrue writes
  `current`, decay writes the blocking `prev`, and a thread runs on at
  most one CPU. Placement reads it only for a non-running RUNNABLE
  thread. The saturating clamp prevents a u32 underflow in `SCALE - u`.
- **#860 is closed by construction, re-confirmed**: the pinned in-tree
  idle is never stealable and always reachable on its home CPU —
  `IdleStaysHome` plus `IdleAvailable`.

## The structural observation

**Every finding lived on the cross-CPU or declared-heterogeneous path,
which is structurally inert on the uniform v1.0 targets** —
`select_target_cpu` returns `prev_cpu` and short-circuits before reading
a capacity. That is precisely why the runtime matrix could not reach any
of them, and why they had to be prosecuted by reasoning. It is the
clearest case in the tree for auditing code the tests cannot execute.
