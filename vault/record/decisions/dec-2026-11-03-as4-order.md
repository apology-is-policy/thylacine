## Fork

Whether async client adoption comes before MM-1 to MM-4, which client adopts first, and where the witness for SAK at every phase lives.

## Decision

Votes 44, 45 and 46 of 2026-11-03: AS-4 first, as `docs/ASYNC-SERVICE-LIFECYCLE.md` orders it, with MM-0 beside it. Nora first, then ut, over `libhalcyon`'s client on the native adapter. A qualification mode in kaua-term-probe stepped by a scenario, plus one Nora scenario.
