A 9P session the kernel killed -- a reply with a tag it never issued, a frame
over its msize -- used to leave the server serving a dead mount that told no
one: nothing hung up until the mount's last close. Now a death hangs up (ARCH
21.10). On the edge of the session's death, `client_mark_dead_locked` calls
the transport's new `hangup` op under `c->lock` ([[sub-kernel-ninep-client]],
[[sub-kernel-ninep-transport]]). Over a pipe that is `pipe_hangup_write`: EOF
without the close, so the server drains what was sent and then reads EOF while
the endpoint stays with the transport ([[sub-kernel-pipe]]). Over a srvconn it
is `srvconn_teardown`, whose locks now nest under `c->lock`
([[sub-kernel-srvconn]], [[lock-9p-client-c-lock]]). A hung-up write end
refuses every later write, and a mounted queue's refusal posts no `pipe` note
(ARCH 10.3, Plan 9's `CMSG` rule), which closes the 2026-08-17 bug in which a
Proc whose 9P operation rode a pipe-mounted session got a SIGPIPE-shaped note
when the server died. haul records the kernel's hangup as `STOP_KERNEL` and
names Thylacine rather than the server; at the attach it asks before closing
its own copy of the pipe, while only the kernel's hangup can raise POLLHUP
([[sub-haul]]). `pipe.tla` gained `HangupWrite`, the action property
`NoByteAfterEof` and two buggy configurations ([[spec-pipe]]). Audit round 1,
Fable 5.1 on Opus 5.5, found nothing above P3; its five P3s were fixed,
including two new attach legs one variable apart. Verified: `tools/test.sh`
1871/1871 and every haul leg on the first attempt; one sabotage per witness,
each red on exactly its designed tests; the two attach legs red only under
their own sabotage, three attempts of three.
