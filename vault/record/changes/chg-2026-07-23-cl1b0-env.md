The kernel writes `envp[0] = NULL` for every program -- the environment
lives in the per-Proc `/env` device (the Plan 9 Egrp idiom, inherited by
children through the kernel clone). A crt hook reads `/env` once at
startup into a `"NAME=value"` vector and points `__environ` at it, so
`getenv` and environ iteration both work.

Placed after `__init_libc` (malloc + TLS up) and before the ctors, so a
constructor's `getenv` sees a populated environ. Fail-soft: a missing
`/env` leaves today's behavior.
