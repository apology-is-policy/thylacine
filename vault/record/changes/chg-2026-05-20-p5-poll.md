## What

Three commits, one day: the mechanism (`poll_waiter` hooks +
register-then-observe + the private-rendez tsleep + `Dev.poll` +
devpipe's implementation + [[spec-poll]]), then devsrv as the second
implementor (listener + connection, the corvus event-loop shape),
then the audit close ([[adt-poll-r1]]: 0 P0 / 2 P1 / 2 P2 / 7 P3).

## The close's two halves

The two P2s were semantics bugs a test could reach
([[fnd-poll-r1-f1]] client-mirror revents,
[[fnd-poll-r1-f2]] the two-lock EOF latch window). The two P1s were
lifetime arguments: [[fnd-poll-r1-f4]] hardened the NULL-obj arm,
and [[fnd-poll-r1-f3]] — the one that matters historically — was
closed by DOCUMENTING a precondition ("single-thread-per-Proc makes
the borrow safe") rather than closing the class. The multi-thread
lift voided the precondition; RW-2 2C-F1 is the same finding
resurfacing as a live UAF, closed structurally
([[chg-2026-06-10-rw2-poll-retain]]). The two-note pair is this
vault's earliest worked example of document-the-precondition vs
close-the-class.
