`Territory.dot_path` + `dot_lock` + the `cwd_lexical_resolve` /
`territory_resolve_cwd` / `getdot` / `setdot` quartet + `SYS_CHDIR = 69`
/ `SYS_GETCWD = 70` + the `SYS_OPEN` relative-to-cwd join. `cd` works.

The design decision that carries the chunk is **name-based, not
handle-based** — the cwd is a cleaned absolute STRING, re-resolved from
`root_spoor` on every relative open, and the deliberate consequence is
that LS-4 adds ZERO new [[inv-i28]] mechanism. The lexical resolver
always hands `stalk` an absolute-from-root path, so `stalk`'s own `..`
clamp becomes a redundant second wall rather than the only one; two
independent clamps compose. Choosing the form that needs no new
containment mechanism was the point, on a resolver where every new
mechanism is a new place to be wrong. Handle-based dot is the v1.x
upgrade and symlinks are its forcing function
([[seam-handle-based-dot]]).

`dot_lock` is a separate leaf from the (then-nonexistent) namespace
lock, guarding one field: readers copy under it, `setdot` swaps under it
and frees the old string outside, `kmalloc` under it is legal because
SLUB has no reverse edge.

Round [[adt-ls4-r1]] came back CLEAN (0/0/0/3, all doc/cosmetic) with a
broad prosecuted-and-unbroken set: no I-28 escape, no `setdot`
UAF/double-free, correct chdir lifetimes, the X-search gate matching
stalk's (and SUPPLYING the final-directory check stalk's `STALK_WALK`
deliberately omits), and `cwd_lexical_resolve` memory-safe under every
input. One P3 ([[fnd-ls4-r1-f3]]) noted the tautological
`source_is_valid` helper as pre-existing and out of scope.
