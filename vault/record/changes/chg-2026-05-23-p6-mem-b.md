`mmap(MAP_ANON)` -> `SYS_BURROW_ATTACH`, `munmap` ->
`SYS_BURROW_DETACH`; file-backed and `MAP_FIXED` refused. The call passes
ONE argument (the length) -- the kernel chooses the VA and the region is
always RW, so `start`/`prot`/`fd`/`off` are stripped at the seam.

mallocng tolerates the loss of `brk` and `mprotect` by its own documented
fallbacks (first `brk(0)` fails -> `ctx.brk = -1` -> metadata via mmap
forever; `mprotect` failure is guarded by `&& errno != ENOSYS`). That
tolerance is also what makes the missing guard pages silent. Retargeted
to `SYS_BURROW_ATTACH_LAZY` at #321 (the overcommit model).
