Sixteen files. The centerpiece is one ioctl dispatcher, because musl's
whole pty/termios surface is ioctl-shaped -- so translating that ONE
function makes `isatty`, `ptsname_r`, `tcgetattr`, `tcsetpgrp`,
`openpty`, and `unlockpt` all work unpatched.

Two changes reach beyond the pty. `openat` moved onto the stalk resolver,
because the 0009 walk loop structurally could not open `/dev/pts/ptmx`
(write-mode, across two mounts) -- a two-month-old latent that only a pty
consumer could surface. And the seven direct `__syscall(SYS_ioctl, ...)`
callers were rerouted through the public `ioctl()`, including the two
stdio line-buffering probes, which is what makes a pouch program's stdout
line-buffered on a terminal.

[[adt-pty3-r1]] closed clean at 3 P3, all sharpenings.
