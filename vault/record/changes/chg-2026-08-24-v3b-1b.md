The V-3b-1b hostmem guest-map landed on main (`215ff750`, hash-fixup `08dd94b2`)
with a holotype that was CLEAN after fixes (Fable 5, family diversity restored;
0 P0 / 0 P1 / 1 P2 / 3 P3, all fixed). [[sub-tapestryd]] gains a "hostmem
guest-map" mechanism section: `HostmemAllocator` (page-aligned bump over
`shm_region(1)`), `hostmem_map_probe` (create + map a HOST3D blob under a venus
ctx -> `PciDev::burrow_from_hostmem` at the HOST-DICTATED cache -> a same-address
u32 sentinel round-trip -> `t_burrow_detach`), and the [[inv-i45]] authority (the
held KObj_PCI claim). The edge worth pinning, and why F1 was a [P2]: the cache
attribute must be **honored exactly** (GPU-DESIGN 6.2) -- the probe passes the
host-dictated `map_info` attribute (CACHED on KVM), never a guess; a guest-WC /
host-WB mismatch is the ARM64 mismatched-alias coherency hazard that would have
surfaced two rungs later at V-3b-1c as stale host ring reads. The
`SYS_BURROW_FROM_HOSTMEM` client wrapper itself (`t_burrow_from_hostmem` +
`T_CACHE_*` in lib.rs, owned by `sub-kernel-syscall-abi`;
`PciDev::burrow_from_hostmem` in hardware.rs, owned by `sub-libthyla-rs`) is a
minor additive binding V-2 left unbuilt -- described in [[sub-tapestryd]] and
here rather than given its own section in those dossiers for a one-fn addition;
a future coverage sweep can promote it if the wrapper grows a surface worth
pinning independently.
