The consumer half of #66: `PgrpMount` gains `mp_path` (the mount POINT's
namespace name, ref-held), `territory_format_ns` renders the mount list,
and `/proc/<pid>/ns` plus a native `ns` tool make a Proc's namespace
readable. `PgrpMount` 32 -> 40 bytes.

The whole chunk is a **four-hook refcount ledger** — `mp_path` must be
ref'd and unref'd at exactly the sites the `source` Spoor already is:
mount's append arm, mount's MREPL displacement (ref-NEW-before-unref-OLD,
so a degenerate shared `Path` survives the swap), unmount's swap-remove
(unref BEFORE the last-entry struct copy overwrites the slot, while the
moved entry's own ref TRANSFERS via the copy), clone's share, and final
release. The self-audit and the Fable round independently derived the
same balance across all five including the clone-OOM rollback.

[[inv-i33]] is what makes the chunk cheap: `mp_path` is grep-complete
NEVER read for a decision — `mount_key_eq`, `mount_is_point_id`,
`would_create_mount_cycle`, and `mount_lookup` all key on
`(mp_dc, mp_devno, mp_qid_path)`, and `territory_format_ns` is the sole
reader. A wrong or NULL `mp_path` misreports one introspection file.
