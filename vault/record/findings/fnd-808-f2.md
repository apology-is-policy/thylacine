## Prosecution

F3's cap: `zone_end = mem_base + 8 GiB`. The direct map:
`l1_directmap[1..8]` = absolute PA [1 GiB, 9 GiB). At
`mem_base == 1 GiB` (QEMU virt) they coincide exactly. At any other
mem_base the relative cap admits PAs above the absolute ceiling —
and `KP_ZERO`'s `pa_to_kva` dereference faults on the first such
page: the original F3 class, reopened by board geometry.
Pre-existing (the cap predates #808); this round surfaced it.

## Disposition

Documented + phys.c carries the full caveat block. The fix is one
line (`min(mem_base + 8 GiB, 9 GiB)`) owed at the first
non-QEMU-geometry bringup — [[seam-mm-directmap-cap-absolute]].
