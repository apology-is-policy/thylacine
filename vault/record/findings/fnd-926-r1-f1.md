## Prosecution

`handle_table_free`'s comment justified its locklessness with "only at
proc_free, thread_count == 0". #926 added a call site on an ALIVE Proc with
a live thread, making the stated premise false.

The real safety was different and unstated: `thread_count <= 1` plus the
fact that no production path touches a FOREIGN ALIVE Proc's handle table.
