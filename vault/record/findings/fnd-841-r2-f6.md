## Prosecution

A departing reader hands the role to B (be_reader + wake); if B's Proc dies
BEFORE assuming it, B returns CLIENT_WAIT_DIED before the election and the
DIED handler did not re-hand-off -- a surviving Proc's pending sleeper is
never elected or woken: a permanent hang on a live session.

## Disposition

Fixed: on the DIED return, `if (rpc->be_reader) { clear;
client_handoff_reader_locked(); }`. The be_reader gate discriminates
(active-reader-death and plain-sleeper-death both have it false -- no
double-hand-off); the re-hand-off chain terminates, bounded by the 64-wide
table. Latent at the v1.0 single-in-flight boot; load-bearing the moment
the client went genuinely multi-in-flight.
