## Prosecution

A Proc dying mid-op drops inflight[tag] but leaves session.outstanding[tag]
active (deliberately -- the I-10 protection against reuse before the
ownerless late reply drains it). A server that accepts the request and
never replies + never EOFs leaks the slot; after 64 such abandons the pool
exhausts and every op on the shared client fails -EIO (fail-safe but
wedged).

## Disposition

Fixed cross-chunk by #845 (Tflush-on-abandon) -- NOT the per-tag
"monotonic generation" first guessed, which 9P's wire cannot carry. The
abandoned tag stays reserved awaiting_flush and frees on its Rflush.
User-voted to implement immediately rather than re-sequence.
