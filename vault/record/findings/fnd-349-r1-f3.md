## Prosecution

A recv error/EOF inside the send path's self-pump marks the whole shared
session dead -- newly reachable from the send path.

## Disposition

Documented as sound: a genuine peer-gone/break IS a death for everyone
(identical to the elected reader's fail-close); the finding's value is the
clarifying comment distinguishing this from the congestion cases that must
NOT latch.
