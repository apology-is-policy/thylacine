## Prosecution

SMP is live during the suite; the kthread on a secondary can GC a
pw==NULL op before the test's `op_count == base+1` snapshot reads --
test-determinism only, the post-close assertions were robust.

## Disposition

Fixed (the prosecutor's endorsed option): the racy snapshot dropped; the
non-racy submission witness (g_cancel_treads, recorded synchronously) +
the post-close assertions kept.
