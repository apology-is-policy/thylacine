## Prosecution

A death-interrupted blocking send returns a partial count. Could a
caller's `write_full` retry loop re-drive the remainder from a dying
context and interleave with teardown?

## Disposition

Documented (in the decl comment): no — the dying Proc unwinds at its
next EL0-return die-check BEFORE any retry can re-enter the kernel, so
the partial count is never re-driven from a live caller. The property is
the #811 death-path contract composing, not a srvconn-local guard.
