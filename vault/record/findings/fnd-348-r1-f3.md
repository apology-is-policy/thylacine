## Prosecution

The producer wait predicate is `count < cap || eof`. Could the eof term
let a woken producer write into a full-and-torn ring?

## Disposition

Documented (clarifying comment at the predicate): no — a producer woken
via eof re-runs `chan_produce`, which checks eof FIRST and fails; the
term exists so teardown unblocks a parked producer, mirroring
`chan_cond_readable`. Contrast deliberately preserved against the ROLE
conds, which carry NO eof term for the opposite reason
([[fnd-cf3b-r1-f2]] — there the term caused a busy-spin); the
keep-one-drop-the-other pairing is on [[sub-kernel-srvconn]]'s
Prosecution list.
