## Prosecution

A handed-off rpc that loses the election race sleeps with be_reader still
set; the wait condition reads be_reader, so sleep returns immediately --
a busy-spin.

## Disposition

Fixed: clear be_reader at the top of the sleep branch (re-woken by the
active reader's departure hand-off; no lost wakeup -- the setter always
pairs set+wakeup on the same rendez). The sound-set note from this round:
be_reader is a pure ADVISORY wake-hint; election is gated solely by
reader_active under c->lock, so two readers are impossible.
