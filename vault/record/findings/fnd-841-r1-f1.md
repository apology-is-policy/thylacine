## Prosecution

read/readdir/readlink dispatch results zero-copy ALIAS into the per-op
`rpc.reply_buf`; `client_run` freed that buffer before the public op copied
the payload out (the old serial path aliased the long-lived transport
recv_buf, so the aliasing habit survived the restructure unexamined). The
caller then copies from freed memory.

## Disposition

Fixed: the `c->done_reply_buf` deferred-free slot -- freed at the next
completion or destroy, under c->lock; holds at most one buffer. Round 2
confirmed SMP-safe (DEAD/DIED paths never stash).
