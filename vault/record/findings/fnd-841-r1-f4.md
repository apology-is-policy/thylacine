## Prosecution

reader_recv_frame loops ops.recv byte-granularly to a full frame -- flagged
for a potential hang.

## Disposition

Accepted: bounded by msize, mirrors do_recv, cannot hang against the
trusted server. (The chunked nature of this loop later became load-bearing
in 8c-3/#90 -- the frame-atomicity work formalized what this finding
observed informally.)
