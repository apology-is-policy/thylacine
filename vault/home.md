# The vault

The knowledge system of Thylacine OS: the as-built technical reference (the
Present plane), the append-only history and adjudication record (the Record
plane), and the generated views that replace every hand-maintained table.
Schema: `vault/meta/schema.md` (binding). Operating loop:
`vault/meta/workflow.md`.

## Where to go

- **Priming for a task**: `system/` — find the surface's `sub-*` dossier;
  follow its `guarded-by` / `hazards` / open-seam links; skim its Provenance.
- **Where are we right now**: `dashboard.md` (generated).
- **The registries**: `invariants/` · `specs/` · `locks/` · `lineages/` ·
  `hazards/` · `glossary/` · `gates/` · `seams/` · `measurements/`.
- **History**: `record/` — arcs contain changes; audits contain findings;
  decisions are the ADRs.
- **Views**: `views/` — audit triggers, the invariant matrix, the seam inbox,
  the roadmap. Generated by `quaestor render`; a stale view fails the
  pre-commit lint.
